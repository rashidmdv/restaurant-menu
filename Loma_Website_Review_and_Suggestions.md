# SITE WEB LOMA: Review and Suggestions

This document provides a summary of the suggested changes to enhance Loma's official website, making it more appealing to customers and aligning with the restaurant's storytelling strategy.

## Site Tree/Site Menu

- **Home** (no in the main menu) - link on logo
- **About us** - the story of the restaurant and its founding values
- **Vision** - a page providing in-depth information on Loma's approach to cuisine and nutrition
- **Chef & Team** - A page dedicated to Matteo Casamichela, his culinary Vision, and his role as leader of the staff
- **Menu** - One webpage that presents the restaurant's cooking proposal
- **Book a table** - form for reservation and restaurant contacts

### NB:

To create an immersive experience for users, the restaurant's atmosphere should be immediately conveyed through images. Therefore, each main page should start with a Hero image, a Carousel display or a short evocative video that captures the mood of the restaurant. Following that, the page structure will repeat three text-image blocks. Below are suggestions for each piece of content.

**Footer:** it must be present on all pages and contains restaurant logo, address, contact details, restaurant opening hours, Legal & Credits (Privacy Policy | Terms & Conditions) + CTA Follow us on social media with icons and links to Loma profiles.
